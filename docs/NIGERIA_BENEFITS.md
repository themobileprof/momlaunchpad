# MomLaunchpad — Benefits for Nigeria

A simple guide for moms, families, and health centres.

**Website:** [momlaunchpad.com](https://momlaunchpad.com) · [igetbelle.com](https://igetbelle.com)  
**Also available:** Android app (install from the website)

---

## In one sentence

MomLaunchpad is a pregnancy companion on your phone — chat for questions, community for support, and tools so you don’t miss appointments.

---

## Why it matters in Nigeria

Many moms:

- Have questions between clinic visits and can’t always reach a midwife or doctor quickly  
- Get advice from many places (WhatsApp groups, relatives, Google) and don’t know what applies to them  
- Miss or forget antenatal appointments and follow-up tests  
- Feel alone — especially when family are far away or work is busy  

MomLaunchpad brings **answers**, **local connection**, and **reminders** into one calm app.

> **Important:** It does **not** replace your midwife, doctor, or hospital. For emergencies, go to the nearest health facility or call emergency services.

---

## Benefits for moms

### 1. Ask questions anytime
Chat with a caring AI companion about symptoms, food, sleep, feelings, or “what should I ask at my next visit?”

**Benefit:** Support at 2am without waiting for your next ANC day.

### 2. Know your week
See a simple week-by-week story of your pregnancy — how baby is growing, what you might feel, and a gentle tip.

**Benefit:** Clear guidance in plain language, not a wall of medical text.

### 3. Don’t miss appointments
Set reminders for antenatal visits, meds, and tests. Optional link to Google Calendar.

**Benefit:** Fewer missed clinic days and less “I forgot.”

### 4. Keep visit notes in one place
Log what the doctor said, next tests, and medicines — so you can review later or share with family.

**Benefit:** Your care story stays with you between facilities.

### 5. Connect with moms near you
Join a community feed based on your city and health centre. Share or read from people who understand your journey.

**Benefit:** Local support, not only distant internet strangers.

### 6. Use it your way
Works for trying to conceive, pregnancy, after birth, and pregnancy loss. Optional WhatsApp number so your health centre can reach you later.

**Benefit:** One app that grows with you.

### 7. Invite friends
Share your referral link. When friends join, you can earn rewards.

**Benefit:** Support spreads through people you trust.

---

## Benefits for health centres & midwives

### 1. Reach moms who already use your facility
When moms select your health centre in the app, you can (after approval) see who’s registered and send gentle in-app announcements.

**Benefit:** Remind them about ANC days, classes, or vaccine days without chasing every phone number yourself.

### 2. Encourage app use — not replace care
MomLaunchpad fills the gap **between** visits. Moms still come to you for clinical care.

**Benefit:** Better-prepared patients; fewer “I didn’t know what to ask” visits.

### 3. Optional WhatsApp contact
Moms can add a phone number. Verified health centre admins can see numbers when shared — useful for WhatsApp follow-up.

**Benefit:** Meet moms where they already communicate.

### 4. Professional presence
Midwives, nurses, and doctors can request a verified badge so moms know who they are talking to in community.

**Benefit:** Trust and visibility for your staff.

## How to start (moms)

1. Open [igetbelle.com](https://igetbelle.com) or [momlaunchpad.com](https://momlaunchpad.com)  
2. Create an account (email or Google)  
3. Tell us your name and pregnancy stage  
4. Optional: add WhatsApp number and choose your health centre  
5. Explore **Home**, **Chat**, **Calendar**, and **Community**

Android: download the app from the website when you want the full phone experience.

---

## How to start (health centres)

1. Staff create a normal MomLaunchpad account  
2. Complete community setup and select your health centre  
3. Open **Health center tools** in Profile and request admin access  
4. MomLaunchpad reviews and approves  
5. Send announcements and view members who registered your centre

## What moms should remember

- Use MomLaunchpad for **everyday support and organisation**  
- Use your **midwife / doctor / hospital** for diagnosis, prescriptions, and emergencies  
- If something feels urgent (heavy bleeding, severe pain, baby not moving, etc.), seek care **immediately**
