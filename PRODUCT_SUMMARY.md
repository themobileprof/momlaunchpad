# MomLaunchpad — Product Summary

**MomLaunchpad** is a pregnancy companion for mothers — available as a **website** and a **mobile app**. It helps moms feel supported, informed, and organized from the moment they start trying to conceive, through pregnancy, after birth, and even through pregnancy loss.

This document explains what the product does in everyday language.

---

## What problem does it solve?

Pregnancy can feel overwhelming. Moms often juggle:

- Questions about their body and baby
- Doctor appointments and reminders
- Feeling alone or unsure where to ask for advice
- Wanting clear, week-by-week guidance without digging through endless articles

MomLaunchpad brings **personal guidance**, **community**, and **practical tools** together in one place — so moms don’t have to bounce between chatbots, calendars, Facebook groups, and random websites.

---

## Who is it for?

MomLaunchpad adapts to where each mom is in her journey:

| Stage | How MomLaunchpad helps |
|--------|-------------------------|
| **Trying to conceive** | Support for fertility questions, waiting, and emotional ups and downs |
| **Pregnant** | Week-by-week baby updates, reminders, visit check-ins, and personalized colors |
| **After birth (postpartum)** | Focus on recovery and the new chapter after delivery |
| **Pregnancy loss** | A gentle, respectful space with support framed for this sensitive time |

Moms can update their journey as life changes — for example, moving from “trying to conceive” to “pregnant.”

---

## The four main pillars

### 1. An AI companion (chat)

Think of this as a caring pregnancy coach you can message anytime.

Moms can start conversations about things like:

- Symptoms they’re worried about
- Nutrition and pregnancy questions
- Preparing for appointments
- How they’re feeling emotionally

They can keep several chat topics going (like separate threads), get thoughtful replies, and even turn a chat suggestion into a calendar reminder.

**In plain terms:** if a mom wakes up at 2am with a question, she has somewhere helpful to ask — without waiting for her next clinic visit.

### 2. A local community

MomLaunchpad includes a community feed where moms can connect with others nearby who share similar interests.

They can:

- Share posts or local events
- Like and reply to others
- Stay anonymous if they prefer
- Hide or report content that doesn’t feel right
- Ask the AI to help them understand a community discussion

**In plain terms:** it’s like a neighborhood parenting circle inside the app — with safety tools built in.

### 3. Week-by-week pregnancy story

When a mom is pregnant, the Calendar area includes a **pregnancy journey** section.

For each week (from early pregnancy through due week), she can see:

- What she might be feeling
- How her baby is developing
- Roughly how big the baby is (for example, “about the size of a banana”)
- A gentle tip
- A simple illustration of development for that month

She can browse previous and next weeks, and quickly jump back to “her” current week.

**In plain terms:** it’s a clear chapter book of pregnancy — one week at a time — instead of a wall of medical text.

### 4. Care logistics (reminders, visits, follow-through)

Pregnancy involves a lot of practical details. MomLaunchpad helps moms stay on top of them:

- **Reminders** for appointments, medications, or anything she wants to remember
- Optional **sync with Google Calendar**, so reminders can also appear in her normal calendar
- **Doctor visit check-ins** on the home screen — gentle nudges before and after appointments
- A place to log visit notes, follow-up tests, or medications discussed with her doctor

**In plain terms:** it helps turn “I should remember that” into something she won’t forget.

## Personalization that makes it feel hers

### Journey stage

The app changes its language and focus based on whether she is trying to conceive, pregnant, postpartum, or navigating loss.

### Baby gender & app colors (optional)

If she knows (or wants to celebrate) her baby’s gender — girl, boy, or “surprise” — she can choose it during setup or later in her profile.

That choice gently changes the app’s color accents (for example, soft rose, ocean blue, or golden plum).

**In plain terms:** the app can feel a little more personal without forcing her to decide.

### Home dashboard

When she opens the app, she sees:

- Where she is in her journey (for example, “Week 20”)
- A personalized weekly welcome note
- Shortcuts to the most useful next steps
- Helpful prompts (like visit check-ins or community highlights)

---

## How moms move through the product

Both the website and the mobile app use a simple bottom menu:

1. **Home** — overview, welcome note, and quick actions  
2. **Calendar** — pregnancy week story (when pregnant) + reminders  
3. **Chat** — AI companion conversations  
4. **Community** — local feed and events  
5. **More** — profile, settings, inbox, and other tools  

This keeps everyday use simple: check in, get guidance, connect, and stay organized.

---

## Website vs mobile app

Most of the core experience is available on **both** platforms. There are a few differences:

### On the website

- Public homepage and invite links (for sharing MomLaunchpad with friends)
- Easier access to a full doctor-visits list
- Staff/admin tools behind the scenes (not for moms)

### On the mobile app

- **Health tracker** for logging symptoms and vitals
- **Hospital bag checklist** (what to pack, with prices and pack status)
- Light / dark appearance settings
- In-app feedback so moms can send suggestions

**Bottom line:** web and mobile are meant to feel like the same product. Mobile adds a few phone-friendly tools for day-to-day health and packing; web is strong for discovery, invites, and full visit records.

---

## Referrals and rewards

Moms can invite friends with a personal link or code.

When friends join, the referring mom can earn reward points and see updates in an inbox (for example, referral rewards or special offers).

**In plain terms:** sharing MomLaunchpad can benefit both the mom who invites and the friend who joins.

---

## Getting started (first-time experience)

When a mom first signs up (email or Google), she is guided through a short setup:

1. Welcome  
2. Her name  
3. Where she is on her journey  
4. A few details (for example pregnancy week, first pregnancy, optional baby gender)

After that, she lands on Home and can explore Chat, Calendar, and Community at her own pace.

Community has a short extra setup (location and interests) so the feed feels relevant to her.

---

## What MomLaunchpad is *not*

To set clear expectations:

- It is **not a replacement for medical care**. The AI and week stories are supportive and educational, not a diagnosis or emergency service.
- It is **not only a content library**. Guidance is conversational and personalized, not just a list of articles.
- It is **not a social network first**. Community is there for local support; the heart of the product is caring guidance plus practical follow-through.

---

## One-sentence summary

**MomLaunchpad is a warm, practical pregnancy companion that helps moms get answers, feel less alone, follow their baby week by week, and stay on top of appointments — on the web and on their phone.**
